# Create engagement score (time spent * completion percentage)
merged_data['Engagement_Score'] = merged_data['Time_Spent_Minutes'] * (merged_data['Completion_Percentage']/100)

Insights

Overall Completion Rate:

--> The average course completion rate is 54.78% ~ 55%, indicating room for improvement in student retention.

Engagement Patterns:

--> Students from Kolkata show the highest engagement (104.38 mins), while Chennai has the lowest (90.94 mins).

--> Younger students (21-25) are slightly more engaged than older age groups.

Course Differences:

--> DM101 has the highest engagement time (102.42 mins) but only the second-highest ratings (2.90).

--> PY202 has the lowest average rating (3.27) despite good engagement time (93.90).

Feedback Analysis:

--> There's a negative correlation (-0.05) between completion rates and feedback ratings.

--> Common feedback themes include courses being "Needing Improvement" and "Too fast-paced".

Top Segments:

--> Others aged 21-25 in Mumbai are the most engaged and satisfied segment.

--> Females aged 31-35 in Mumbai also show high engagement and satisfaction.

Recommendations

Course Content Review:

--> Focus on PY202 improvements as it has the lowest ratings despite good engagement.

--> Address the "Too fast-paced" feedback by offering adjustable learning speeds or supplemental materials.

Regional Engagement Strategies:

--> Investigate why Chennai has lower engagement and develop targeted engagement strategies.

--> Replicate successful engagement approaches from Kolkata in other regions.

Personalized Learning Paths:

--> Create tailored content for high-performing segments (e.g., Others age ranging between 21-25 in Mumbai).
